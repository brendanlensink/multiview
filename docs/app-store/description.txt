Coverage turns the iPhones you already have into a synced multi-camera rig — no extra hardware, no accounts, no internet connection required.

Pick one phone as the Director and turn any other nearby iPhones into Cameras. The Director finds them automatically over your local network, shows every live feed in a grid, and starts and stops recording on all of them with a single tap. Every angle is captured with synchronized timestamps, so it's ready to cut together the moment you're done.

HOW IT WORKS
• Open the app, choose Director or Camera — that's the whole setup.
• Camera devices appear on the Director automatically. One tap to connect.
• The Director shows a live grid of every connected angle at all times.
• Start recording once, and every device records in sync.
• Stop recording, then export as a single split-screen composite or as separate files per angle, saved straight to your camera roll.

WHY COVERAGE
• No accounts, no sign-in, no cloud — everything runs peer-to-peer over your local network.
• No dedicated capture hardware — just the iPhones already in the room.
• Grid composite export gives you an instant multi-angle video, no editing required.
• Separate-file export hands you synced clips ready to drop into your NLE of choice.
• Adjustable capture quality (resolution and bitrate) to balance video fidelity against network conditions.

GREAT FOR
• Podcasts and interviews — capture every speaker's angle at once.
• Vlogs and tutorials — a front angle and a hands angle, recorded together.
• Family moments and events — cover more of the room without missing a beat.
• Behind-the-scenes and product shoots — multiple angles without a video village.

Coverage works with a few connected cameras at once (practical limits depend on your local network and device hardware), streaming live over Wi-Fi with no data ever leaving your devices.
